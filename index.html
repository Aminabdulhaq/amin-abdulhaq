INTRODUCTION

Entrepreneur and investor with a competitive tennis career.

Amin Abdulhaq is an entrepreneur and investor based in Cairo, Egypt, with experience in commercial development, business operations, company-building and international competitive tennis. This website is his official professional profile.

His career began in corporate sales and business development and progressed into project management at Coca-Cola HBC Egypt. He founded Aminco Car Services in 2017 and Kingdom of Aminco in 2019, and holds investment interests including STCO Group and MAC Investments.

Away from business, he started playing tennis in 2021 and has since represented Yemen in the Davis Cup. His career-high ITF Masters Tour singles ranking is 412, reached on 17 March 2025.
