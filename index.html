INTRODUCTION

Entrepreneurship before the businesses.

Amin Abdulhaq is an entrepreneur and investor based in Cairo, Egypt, with experience in commercial development, business operations, company-building and international competitive tennis.

His interest in business began while studying in California, where he sourced, repaired and resold mobile phones internationally. Back in Egypt he built a career at Coca-Cola from sales to development project management, becoming the number-one salesperson in Cairo in 2016 and working on operational ideas in cashless collections, night-time loading and third-party distribution.

Alongside that career he built his first major business, Aminco Car Services, reinvested the profits, and expanded into complementary activities: trucking and logistics, construction, mobile car washing, service vans and automotive care. Today his portfolio includes Aminco Car Services, Kingdom of Aminco and, as a newer venture, Queen Sheba Honey.
