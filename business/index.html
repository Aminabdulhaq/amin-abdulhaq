Amin Abdulhaq: Entrepreneurship and Business Portfolio

Three business pillars (automotive services, logistics and premium consumer products) designed to support one another.

An interconnected business ecosystem

Amin's approach to entrepreneurship moved beyond founding individual companies. After building his first major business and reinvesting its profits, he invested in complementary activities that could support one another operationally. The portfolio is designed as a connected service-centre and logistics ecosystem, not a list of unrelated companies. The full story is on the journey page.

Aminco Car Services

Founded in 2017, Aminco Car Services covers automotive care, car washing, detailing and mobile automotive services. Its service vans take the work to the customer, and its service-centre operations sit alongside other offerings. The Aminco Group website presents Aminco Mobile Car Care, an exterior mobile car wash serving Uptown Cairo and nearby areas.

Visit Aminco Group

Kingdom of Aminco

Founded in 2019, Kingdom of Aminco is a logistics and transportation business covering trucking, distribution and FMCG distribution. Its model uses a combination of owned and subcontracted vehicle capacity. The thinking behind it draws on Amin's business development years at Coca-Cola, where he worked on hotspot distribution and third-party transport.

Logistics & Transportation

Logistics is the pillar that moves goods for the others: trucking and transportation capacity, FMCG distribution, and the planning that keeps vehicles loaded and ready.

Photograph shown for illustration of fleet operations. For Amin's Coca-Cola distribution experience, see the career page.

Construction

Amin has also retained construction business activities as part of his wider entrepreneurial journey. Construction complements the service and logistics operations as one more area where shared resources, transportation and relationships can create efficiencies.

Queen Sheba Honey

A developing venture

Amin and his brother established a business relationship with one of the largest Yemeni honey distributors to develop a premium Yemeni honey brand: Queen Sheba Honey. The focus is on sourcing some of Yemen's rarest and highest-quality Sidr honey and building a premium brand for the Egyptian market, with distribution starting in Cairo.

The longer-term ambition is to expand distribution internationally. Queen Sheba Honey is a newer venture that extends the portfolio into premium Yemeni products.
