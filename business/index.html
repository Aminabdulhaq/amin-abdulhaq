Amin Abdulhaq: Entrepreneurship and Business Portfolio

Operating businesses founded by Amin Abdulhaq in Cairo, built alongside a corporate career.

Operating businesses

These are companies Amin founded and built. They are presented separately from his investment interests, which are shareholdings in other groups.

Aminco Car Services

Founded 2017

Founded as an independent automotive services business focused on vehicle care and fleet support. The Aminco Group website also presents Aminco Mobile Car Wash, an exterior mobile car wash serving Uptown Cairo and nearby areas.

Kingdom of Aminco

Founded 2019

A logistics, transportation and FMCG distribution business. It delivers through a combination of owned and subcontracted vehicle capacity, and is described here without claims about fleet size.

Queen Sheba Honey

Part of Aminco Group: premium Yemeni Sidr honey, imported into Egypt and offered through the Aminco Group website, with orders placed over WhatsApp.

Aminco Group

Aminco Group is the umbrella under which these businesses are presented online at amincocars.com, where customers can find car care, logistics and honey in one place.
