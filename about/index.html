Amin Abdulhaq: Biography

Entrepreneur, investor and international tennis competitor: the background, career and interests behind the name.

Yemeni roots, a professional life in Egypt

Amin Abdulhaq comes from a Yemeni family and has built his professional life largely in Egypt, where he is based in Cairo. His career has crossed borders: between 2013 and 2016 he held a business-development support role with a Sudan-based company while working from Egypt and travelling to Sudan, and his businesses today connect Egypt and Yemen, including Yemeni Sidr honey distributed through Aminco Group.

An early commercial instinct

His interest in business began while he was still studying, when he started an independent trading venture buying and reselling damaged iPhones through international markets. The experience introduced him to sourcing, price differences between markets, negotiation and commercial risk, lessons that carried into his later career.

From frontline sales to project management

Amin's corporate career began in sales in 2013. He progressed into business development, becoming Business Development Manager from April 2015, and later worked as Development Project Manager, his final documented position at Coca-Cola HBC Egypt. Along the way he gained experience in commercial development, distribution and warehouse processes. The dates and responsibilities are set out on the career page.

Becoming an entrepreneur

He founded Aminco Car Services in 2017 and Kingdom of Aminco, a logistics, transportation and distribution business, in 2019. His documented employment at Coca-Cola HBC Egypt continued until April 2023, so these periods overlap in time. Both businesses are described in the business portfolio.

Investments and business development

Separately from the companies he founded, Amin holds investment interests, including as a shareholder in STCO Group, which his professional profile dates from January 2010, and in MAC Investments. These are shareholder and investment interests rather than executive positions; see investment interests.

Competitive tennis

Amin began playing tennis in 2021 with no previous playing experience. He progressed to the ITF Masters Tour, reaching a career-high singles ranking of 412 on 17 March 2025, and has represented Yemen in the Davis Cup. His official records are linked on the tennis page.

Family heritage, and a path of his own

Amin is the grandson of the late Yemeni businessman Shaher Abdulhak. He acknowledges the influence of that heritage, but this biography distinguishes between his family's historical business background and his own professional record: his employment, the businesses he founded, his investment interests and his sporting career. The heritage page covers this in more detail.

Key dates
