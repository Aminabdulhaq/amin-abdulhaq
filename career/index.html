Three operational innovations

During his business development years, Amin worked on three ideas that improved how the business collected money and moved product. Each began with a practical inefficiency.

01

Cashless collections through Fawry

The problem
The business relied heavily on cash collections, which created significant cash discrepancies and reconciliation problems.
The idea
Introduce cashless transactions through Fawry in the sales collection process, giving a more controlled way to collect payment.
The aim
Reduce discrepancies and improve financial accountability. At the time, it was an innovative operational improvement.
02

Night loading, early-morning deliveries

The idea
Park and load trucks at night so they can leave early in the morning for deliveries.
Why it helps
  • Operational readiness: trucks start the day already prepared.
  • Loading efficiency: loading is no longer squeezed into the morning rush.
  • Distribution preparation: deliveries can begin earlier.
03

Hotspots and third-party transport

The idea
Use strategically located hotspots, so company-owned trucks do not have to travel everywhere. Product moves onward through third-party transport providers at negotiated, attractive rates that still let those operators earn a profit.
The logic
  • Better utilisation of transport capacity
  • Less reliance on company trucks for every route
  • Faster product movement
  • Improved distribution efficiency
  • Commercial value through negotiated rates and partnerships

What this experience built

These initiatives show a working knowledge of business development, logistics, financial controls, distribution strategy, operational efficiency and commercial innovation. They also show a pattern: spot an inefficiency, then build a practical solution around it. That habit carries into the businesses Amin founded.
