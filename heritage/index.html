Amin Abdulhaq: Family Heritage and Business Legacy

A respectful look at the legacy of the late Shaher Abdulhak, and how Amin Abdulhaq has built his own career.

Shaher Abdulhak

Amin is the grandson of the late Yemeni businessman Shaher Abdulhak. His family heritage forms part of his background and his connection to a long-standing commercial legacy in Yemen.

A heritage acknowledged, a path built independently

Amin recognises the influence of that heritage on his outlook. At the same time, his own career has been developed through independent work. This site keeps the two separate:

  • Family background: the historical business legacy of Shaher Abdulhak, described with respect and without attributing it to Amin.
  • Amin's own record: his Coca-Cola career, the businesses he founded, his investment interests and his international tennis career.

The heritage also connects to the newest venture in his portfolio, Queen Sheba Honey, which draws on Yemeni sourcing and heritage.
